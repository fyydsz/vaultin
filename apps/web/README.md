> A personal money manager and savings tracker that actually gets out of your way.

Most finance apps are either cluttered with ads and subscriptions, or too complex for daily use. **Vaultin** is built for people who want clear control over their money—setting aside funds for real goals, seeing where daily cash actually goes, and staying on top of monthly budgets without feeling overwhelmed.

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do with Vaultin

### 1. Goal-Based Savings (Vaults)
Instead of keeping all your savings in one generic lump sum, divide your money into dedicated vaults:
- Set specific targets for things like **Emergency Fund**, **Vacation**, **Gadget Upgrades**, or **Tax Reserves**.
- Set optional target deadlines to see your pace.
- Log deposits and withdrawals anytime with quick notes to see how your balance grew over time.

### 2. Cashflow & Monthly Overview
Get a real look at your monthly income vs. spending without getting lost in spreadsheets:
- **Income vs. Expense charts**: Spot at a glance whether you are saving or overspending in any given month.
- **Category breakdown**: Visual breakdowns showing how much went to essentials, dining out, bills, entertainment, or shopping.
- **Monthly trends**: Compare month-to-month habits so you can adjust your plans before the month ends.

### 3. Fast Transaction Tracking & Smart Filters
Logging day-to-day transactions shouldn't take more than a few seconds:
- Record expenses, income, or transfers between accounts with minimal clicks.
- Tag transactions with color-coded categories and custom labels.
- **Deep search & multi-filters**: Quickly find past transactions by date range, category, type, or nominal amount range (min/max).

### 4. Multi-Account & Wallet Management
Keep an accurate pulse on all your money spots in one place:
- Track cash on hand, bank accounts, and digital wallets.
- Overview your total net balance with automatic calculations as transactions happen.

### 5. Clean, Distraction-Free Experience
- **Dark & Light mode**: Easy on the eyes day or night.
- **Mobile-friendly**: Designed to work smoothly on your phone's browser when you're paying at a cashier or out with friends.
- **Customizable profile**: Update your avatar with built-in crop and personalize your workspace.

---

## 💡 The Idea Behind Vaultin

Vaultin started with a simple problem: spreadsheet fatigue. Tracking finances in Excel works until it becomes a chore, and most third-party apps want your bank credentials or spam you with loan offers. 

Vaultin focuses strictly on what matters: **giving you a clear, honest snapshot of your finances so you can save with intention.**
